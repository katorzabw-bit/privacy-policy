VaultX Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to VaultX, a secure digital vault designed to protect passwords, files, notes, payment card information, and sensitive personal data through advanced encryption technologies.

By using VaultX, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

2. Zero-Knowledge Security Architecture

VaultX follows a Zero-Knowledge Architecture.

All sensitive vault content is encrypted locally on your device before storage, synchronization, backup, or transfer.

VaultX developers, employees, servers, cloud providers, and third parties cannot read, access, or decrypt your vault contents.

Encryption keys remain under your control and are never transmitted in plain form.

---

3. Information We Store

Depending on the features you use, VaultX may store:

- Passwords and login credentials
- Usernames and account information
- Secure notes
- Payment card information
- Encrypted files and documents
- Encrypted images and media
- OCR extracted text
- Security preferences
- Language preferences
- Backup and synchronization settings

Sensitive vault content is encrypted before storage.

---

4. Authentication

VaultX supports:

- Google Sign-In
- Email and Password Authentication

Authentication information is used solely for account access, synchronization, backup management, and security features.

---

5. Cloud Backup and Synchronization

If cloud synchronization is enabled:

- Encrypted data may be stored using Firebase services.
- Encrypted backups may be stored in Google Drive if linked by the user.
- Only encrypted information is synchronized.
- VaultX cannot decrypt your vault contents.

Data transmission occurs through secure HTTPS/TLS connections.

---

6. Encryption Standards

VaultX uses strong modern encryption technologies including:

- AES-256-GCM encryption
- Android Keystore
- EncryptedSharedPreferences
- Biometric authentication integration
- Secure HTTPS/TLS transport

Files, images, passwords, notes, and vault content are encrypted before storage whenever applicable.

---

7. Autofill Service

VaultX may provide Android Autofill functionality.

The Autofill service operates locally on the user's device and may store encrypted credential data required for autofill operations.

Credential matching and retrieval occur locally whenever possible.

---

8. OCR Scanner

VaultX includes Optical Character Recognition (OCR) features powered by local OCR processing technologies.

Images selected by the user may be processed locally on the device to extract text.

VaultX does not intentionally upload OCR image content for text recognition purposes unless explicitly required by a user-selected cloud feature.

---

9. Biometrics

VaultX supports:

- Fingerprint Authentication
- Face Authentication

Biometric data never leaves your device.

VaultX receives only the authentication result provided by the operating system and does not store biometric templates.

11. Analytics and Diagnostics

VaultX may use:

- Firebase Analytics
- Firebase Crashlytics

These services may collect limited technical information such as:

- App version
- Device model
- Operating system version
- Crash reports
- Performance metrics

Encrypted vault contents are never included in diagnostic reports.

---

12. Advertising

VaultX may display advertisements through Google AdMob.

Advertising partners may collect certain information according to their own privacy policies.

VaultX does not share encrypted vault contents, passwords, files, notes, or encryption keys with advertisers.

---

13. Data Retention

Data remains available until:

- The user deletes it,
- The user deletes their account,
- The user removes backups,
- Or the user uninstalls the application and removes local storage.

---

14. Data Deletion

Users may:

- Delete vault entries
- Delete encrypted files
- Remove cloud backups
- Clear local vault data
- Delete their account

Deleted information may become unrecoverable unless a valid encrypted backup exists.
